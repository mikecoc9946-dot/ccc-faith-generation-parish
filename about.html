About Our Parish

Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish (Ebute Iyanju)

WELCOME

A community rooted in faith

Welcome to Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish, also known as Ebute Iyanju, located in Ijoko, Ogun State, Nigeria. Our parish is a place where people come together for worship, prayer, fellowship, and spiritual encouragement.

We invite you to learn more about our church, take part in our programmes, and connect with our parish community.

Our Mission

To worship God, share the message of Jesus Christ, and encourage spiritual growth through prayer, fellowship, and service.

Our Vision

To be a welcoming faith community where people are encouraged to grow in faith and reflect the love of Christ.

Our Values

Faith, prayer, worship, love, fellowship, service, and commitment to the teachings of Christ.

Where to find us

Olorunda Estate, Ntabo Road, Ijoko, Ogun State, Nigeria.
