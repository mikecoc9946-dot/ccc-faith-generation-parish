ABOUT US

Welcome to Faith Generation Parish

Welcome to Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish, Ijoko, Ogun State. We are a family of believers united in faith, prayer, worship and love.

Our desire is to see lives transformed through the word of God, the power of prayer and the fellowship of believers. We invite you to worship with us and experience the goodness of God.

✦

Our Faith

We believe in the power of God, prayer, holiness and the teachings of Jesus Christ.

✦

Our Mission

To worship God, spread His word, serve humanity and encourage spiritual growth.

✦

Our Community

A welcoming family where people come together in love, faith, unity and fellowship.
