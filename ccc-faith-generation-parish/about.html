About Us

Discover our faith, mission, vision and the heart of our parish.

Welcome to Faith Generation Parish

Welcome to Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish, also known as Ebute Iyanju. We are a community of believers committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, living in love and spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our parish is a place of prayer, spiritual growth, fellowship, hope and transformation. We welcome everyone seeking God's presence, guidance, healing and blessings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire lives and strengthen faith through the messages of hope, love, truth and the word of God. We are committed to spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ, nurturing spiritual growth, encouraging prayer and building a community where everyone can experience God's presence and purpose.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a spiritually vibrant and Christ-centered community where lives are transformed, faith is strengthened, love is demonstrated and the gospel reaches people everywhere. We desire to raise believers who live according to God's word and serve Him faithfully.

Our Core Values

🙏 Faith & Hope

We believe in God and trust His promises, encouraging everyone to live with faith, hope and confidence in Him.

❤️ Love & Compassion

We demonstrate the love of Christ through kindness, compassion, forgiveness and care for one another.

🤝 Community & Growth

We build a welcoming community where people grow spiritually, support one another and serve God together.
