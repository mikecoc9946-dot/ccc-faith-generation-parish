Welcome to Faith Generation Parish

Welcome to Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish, located in Ijoko, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are a community of believers committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, living in love, and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

We welcome everyone to join us in worship, prayer, fellowship, and the study of God's Word as we journey together in faith and discover God's purpose for our lives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to worship God in spirit and truth, preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers in faith, and serve our community with love, compassion, and humility.

Through prayer, teaching, fellowship, and service, we seek to inspire lives and strengthen faith.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a spiritually strong and united community of believers who live according to God's Word, grow in holiness, and shine the light of Christ to the world.

We desire to see lives transformed and people drawn closer to God through faith, love, and truth.

Our Core Values

🙏

Faith & Hope

We believe faith in God and hope in His promises are the foundations of a life of strength, peace, and spiritual growth.

💖

Love & Compassion

We embrace the love of Christ and show kindness, compassion, respect, and care to everyone.

🌱

Community & Growth

We grow together through fellowship, prayer, learning God's Word, supporting one another, and building a united church family.
