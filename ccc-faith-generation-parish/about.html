Welcome to Faith Generation Parish

Welcome to Celestial Church of Christ, Faith Generation Parish, located in Ijoko, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are a community of believers committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, living in love, and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

We welcome you to join us in worship, prayer, fellowship, and the study of God's Word.

Our Mission

To worship God in spirit and truth, preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers in faith, and serve our community with love, compassion, and humility.

Our Vision

To build a spiritually strong and united community of believers who live according to God's Word, grow in holiness, and shine the light of Christ to the world.
